Debrief prompts

Phase 1 → 2: Did cooperation or average payoffs change when you knew you would face the same opponent again? Why?

Phase 2 → 3: What did chat change? Did verbal promises reliably predict choices?

Economics → law: Can firms achieve similar outcomes without explicit agreement? Why are price-fixing agreements prohibited under Article 101 TFEU?

Limitations: Payoffs are fixed, industries differ, and a scripted bot or small sample can bias outcomes. Distinguish strategic incentives from causal proof.
